How It Works

We've streamlined personal lending into six simple, secure stages. Learn exactly what happens from your initial application to final funding.

1

Complete Online Application

Submit your loan details, personal details, employment information, and banking consent securely through our encrypted wizard.

2

Verify Your Identity

Our secure automated portal verifies your identity against regulatory databases to keep your details safe from identity fraud.

3

Secure Document Upload

Provide verification documents (like photo IDs, recent paystubs, or tax forms) directly into your secure portal. Your files are encrypted at rest.

4

Underwriting Review

Our dedicated lending officers review your application, scoring risk factor models transparently while keeping your privacy prioritized.

5

Approval & Terms

Receive your formal lending decision. If approved, you can review rates, select your term duration, and sign loan papers electronically.

6

Direct Deposit Funding

Once finalized, funds are deposited directly into your linked bank account, typically arriving in 1-2 business days.
